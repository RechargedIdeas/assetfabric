# Acceptance — F6 Vertical slice: quests, farming/craft economy, tiered loot, adaptive music, cave ↔ open world

**Fabric delivers:** Codegen-World (`content/world/*.yaml` → `data/generated/world.json`: economy — items, crops, recipes, shop, XP; tiered loot tables; quests compiled to one parallel statechart; adaptive-music rules; area graph) with gates for schema, cross-refs, quest-chart soft-lock, economy solvability, loot tables and music rules; G1-Tile open-world features (farm plots, workbench, gather nodes, pinned spawns); Player State save **v4** (farm, checkpoint, quest counters, rng, buffs, gear, unlocks) with v1→v4 migrations in GDScript ↔ Python parity; Python twins of quest log and loot rolls; adaptive music director. (SPEC §13 F6, GAME_SPEC §4.4, §5, §6.1)
**Game proves:** the **vertical slice** (GAME_SPEC §6.1) — select → farm → tunnels → deep cave → farm → craft → Cave Warden → open-world exit → save — played end-to-end headless by a scripted QA autopilot for **all 12** leader × starter-pet selections, plus save/quit/reload in a new process.

Step 0 (balance carry-over from F5): pet↔character affinity (GAME_SPEC §4.2) and the **per-pair** G-BAL-01 gate — see §Balance.

### Length (gated on the human-pace estimate since 0.7.12 — ADR-0017)

GAME_SPEC §6.1 targets **15–20 minutes** of continuous play. `forge accept F6` gates the **human-pace estimate**
(`forge/pace.py`, `content/tune/pace.yaml`, INFERENCE C-92) for the main run *and every one of the 12 selections*:
**17.26 min** on the acceptance selection, **17.03–18.51 min** across the matrix (`perf.human_estimate_min`,
`perf.matrix_human_estimate_min`, `perf.length_within_target = true`). Components of the main run: travel 481 s,
combat 85 s, exploring 6 new areas 180 s, interactions 171 s, reading 53 s, select 45 s, swap overlays 20 s, crop wait
0 s (the bot's 68 s of waiting is absorbed by the slower human pace since planting).

The QA autopilot itself finishes in **7.10 game-minutes** (25 550 ticks @ 60 Hz), **7.10–7.35** across the matrix —
reported as-is (`perf.bot_length_within_target = false`); it was 2.92 before Q4. The difference is content, not a
slower bot: Ore Hollow, the patrol beat during the second crop cycle, the q_smith side quest and Windward Ridge
(INFERENCE C-91). The estimate is a model: the playtest below replaces it, and if people finish well under 15 min the
fix is more content, not new constants (ADR-0017).

## Balance (step 0 — per-pair G-BAL-01)

F5 gated only the choice marginals; the 12 leader × pet cells spread **12.7 pp**. F6 adds a per-(pet, leader) **affinity** multiplier on the pet's own-leader bonus (`content/roster/pets.yaml`, live-tune bounds 0.25–3.0, 0–3 since Q5) and makes the per-cell spread a **hard check** (`max_combo_deviation_lt_5pp`) [INFERENCE F6-1, F6-2].

| Seeds | Role | Mean | Choice dev | Per-pair dev | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| seed_base 1000 × 200/cell (2400) | acceptance (`forge accept F5`), partly in-sample | 0.718 | 1.04 pp | **3.21 pp** (0.690–0.750) | ✅ pass |
| seed_base 9000 × 200/cell (2400) | unseen hold-out (reported) | 0.708 | 1.92 pp | **5.25 pp** (0.665–0.760) | would fail |

At 200 seeds/cell the per-cell standard error is ≈ 3.2 pp, so 12 perfectly balanced cells show ≈ 5–6 pp max deviation on fresh seeds: the hold-out is at the noise floor. A meaningful out-of-sample per-cell gate needs ~800 seeds/cell (needs a faster worker).

**Q5 (0.7.13):** closed with a powered hold-out — 1000 unseen seeds/cell (seed_base 20 000) gated at ≤ 3.5 pp in full
`forge accept F5`: **2.71 pp** after recalibrating three affinities (tortle→nyra 1.0, wisp→sola 0.0, tortle→sola 0.77;
affinity range now 0–3). The 200-seed check became noise-aware (`combo_ci_within_5pp`). Details:
[F5.md §Q5](F5.md#q5--per-pair-balance-on-unseen-seeds-0713-2026-11-27-reference-box-8-vcpu), INFERENCE C-94.
