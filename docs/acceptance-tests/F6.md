## Balance (step 0 — per-pair G-BAL-01)

F5 gated only the choice marginals; the 12 leader × pet cells spread **12.7 pp**. F6 adds a per-(pet, leader) **affinity** multiplier on the pet's own-leader bonus (`content/roster/pets.yaml`, live-tune bounds 0.25–3.0) and makes the per-cell spread a **hard check** (`max_combo_deviation_lt_5pp`) [INFERENCE F6-1, F6-2].

| Seeds | Role | Mean | Choice dev | Per-pair dev | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| seed_base 1000 × 200/cell (2400) | acceptance (`forge accept F5`), partly in-sample | 0.718 | 1.04 pp | **3.21 pp** (0.690–0.750) | ✅ pass |
| seed_base 9000 × 200/cell (2400) | unseen hold-out (reported) | 0.708 | 1.92 pp | **5.25 pp** (0.665–0.760) | would fail |

At 200 seeds/cell the per-cell standard error is ≈ 3.2 pp, so 12 perfectly balanced cells show ≈ 5–6 pp max deviation on fresh seeds: the hold-out is at the noise floor. A meaningful out-of-sample per-cell gate needs ~800 seeds/cell (needs a faster worker).
